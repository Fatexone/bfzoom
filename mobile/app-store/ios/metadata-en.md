## App Name
BFZoom

## Subtitle
Video, chat, and AI training

## Promotional Text
Meet, chat, and improve language skills in one app: video calls, smart chat, and AI training. Some live translation and AI features require in-app purchase translation packs.

## Keywords
video,chat,translation,languages,training,captions,meeting,call,learning,ai

## Description
BFZoom combines video conferencing, chat, and language training in one app.

Important:
- The app can be downloaded and explored for free.
- Some live translation and AI-powered features require translation packs available as in-app purchases.
- Translation minutes are shared between the iPhone app and the web experience when the same account is used.

Multilingual video conferencing
- Live captions during video calls.
- Voice translation to improve cross-language conversations. Translation during calls uses translation minutes.
- Simple controls designed for mobile and desktop.

Smart chat
- Direct and group conversations.
- Smoother communication between users speaking different languages.
- Discussion space connected to your sessions.

Interactive training
- Personalized lesson generation.
- Guided practice to improve faster.
- Continuous learning at your own pace.
- Some AI practice flows and audio features use translation minutes.

Who is it for?
- International teams
- Students and self-learners
- Anyone who wants to communicate without language barriers

BFZoom helps you communicate, learn, and collaborate every day.
